# Image credits

## Placeholder imagery — REMOVE BEFORE LAUNCH

The files prefixed `wm-` in `/public` come from Wikimedia Commons. They are in
place **only to preview the design with varied artwork** while Solange's own
photography is pending.

**They must not ship as published work.** Presenting third-party conservation
photography on a conservator's portfolio reads as claiming someone else's work,
and a curator who recognises a Commons image would reasonably distrust
everything else on the site — including the credentials, which are real. Replace
each one as soon as real photography exists, then delete this section.

While they remain, the CC BY-SA licences below oblige us to credit the authors.
That obligation is live from the moment the site is public.

**Note on CC BY-SA:** attribution alone may not be sufficient. Share-alike can
extend to derivative works, and cropping or compositing these images could pull
parts of the site into the same licence. It is a real question for a commercial
site, and another reason these are a preview device rather than a solution.

The public-domain van Dyck carries no restriction and could stay indefinitely if
it earns its place.
